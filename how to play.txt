| **OpenWeather API Key** | Optional: Add your key to `OPENWEATHER_API_KEY` in [`server/.env`](file:///Users/deepesh/Documents/Projects/itenary/server/.env). | If left blank, intelligent weather recommendations still function with simulated conditions. |
